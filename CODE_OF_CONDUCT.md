# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is expected

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience, background or first
  language. Contributors here do not all write English natively; read for
  meaning, not for grammar.
- Give feedback about the work. "This breaks on an empty script" is useful;
  "this is sloppy" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way you would want it given.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Nobody keeps score.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer at **bozdogancanahmet@gmail.com**. Reports
are handled privately and in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that do not fit these rules, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone
whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Where a reason can be
given, it will be.
